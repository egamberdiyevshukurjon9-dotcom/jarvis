---
name: revenue
description: Sales and revenue — today, yesterday, this month, compared with before.
requires: connectors
---
- Use a payments connector (Stripe, PayPal, a shop's dashboard) if present;
  otherwise open the dashboard in the user's own Chrome and read the figures there.
- Answer with the number and its direction: "Revenue yesterday was four hundred and
  twenty dollars, sir, up twelve percent on the day before."
- On a blade, show a small table: period, revenue, number of payments, change.
  Refunds and failed payments get their own line if there are any.
- Never issue a refund, change a price or move money. If asked, say what would be
  done and do it only with writes on and after one confirming sentence.
