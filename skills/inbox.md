---
name: inbox
description: Unread mail, the three most important, and who is still waiting for a reply.
requires: connectors
---
- Use the mail connector (Gmail, Outlook) if present; otherwise read the inbox in
  the user's own Chrome.
- Importance, highest first: a person (not a newsletter, receipt or notification)
  who is a client, a colleague or someone the user has written to before; anything
  with a date, a deadline, money or a question addressed to the user; then age.
- "Waiting for a reply": threads where the last message is from someone else, is
  addressed to the user directly, asks something, and is older than two days.
  Report who and how long ("Aziz has waited three days").
- Speak the count and the single most important item. The three most important,
  and the waiting list, go on a blade: sender, subject, one-line gist, age.
- Never read a whole email aloud unless asked. Never send, archive, delete or mark
  as read without being told to; drafting a reply is fine, sending needs writes
  and a one-sentence confirmation first.
