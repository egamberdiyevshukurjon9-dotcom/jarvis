---
name: morning-briefing
description: Good morning, what do I have tomorrow, or the scheduled morning briefing.
requires: connectors
---
Triggered by "good morning", "brief me", "what do I have today / tomorrow", or the
message "Morning briefing" that the interface sends on its own at the set time.

1. Calendar first. Read the day asked about (today in the morning, tomorrow in the
   evening). Follow the calendar skill to find clashes.
2. Then the inbox. Follow the inbox skill: the unread count, the three that matter
   most, and anyone who has waited more than two days for a reply.
3. If a payments or sales tool is connected, add yesterday's revenue in one clause.
4. Put it all on ONE blade with `display`: a timeline of the day (time, title,
   where — Meet, Teams, Zoom, room), clashes highlighted, then the three emails,
   then the unanswered ones marked "no reply".
5. Speak at most three sentences: how many meetings and the first one, the clash
   if there is one, and the one email or reply that cannot wait.
   Example: "Four meetings tomorrow, sir, the first at seven thirty. The client
   call at one thirty overlaps the team meeting. You have not answered the client's
   letter from Tuesday."
6. If neither a calendar nor a mail tool is reachable, open them in the user's own
   Chrome with the chrome tools (calendar.google.com, mail.google.com, or
   outlook.live.com) and read them there. Never send, accept or decline anything as
   part of a briefing.
